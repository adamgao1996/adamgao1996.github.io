🎉 **"3D Point Splatting for mmWave Radar Novel View Synthesis"** by Adnan Armouti, Yixuan Gao, and Rajalakshmi Nandakumar has been accepted to the **NeurIPS 2026 Main Track** (acceptance rate 25.7%)! 3DPS is the first differentiable point renderer for radar: one optimized scene renders ADC, complex range profile, and range-azimuth outputs, with about 3 minutes of training per scene. [arXiv:2609.11894](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11894)
