📄 New preprint **"mmFHE: mmWave Sensing with End-to-End Fully Homomorphic Encryption"** by Tanvir Ahmed, Yixuan Gao, Adnan Armouti, and Rajalakshmi Nandakumar is now on arXiv. mmFHE is the first end-to-end FHE system for mmWave radar sensing, with negligible utility loss compared to plaintext processing.
