🎉 **"RadarAgent: Agentic Tool Utilization for Radar-to-Language Understanding"** by Shuang He, Yixuan Gao, and Rajalakshmi Nandakumar has been accepted to **HumanSys 2026** (Austin, TX)! RadarAgent lets a foundation model call radar-processing tools to describe human motion in natural language, with no radar-token pretraining.
